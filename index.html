Needle Drops

A "needle drop" is film-editor slang for dropping an existing recording onto a scene — as opposed to a composer scoring one from scratch. This is a list of 100 needle drops (plus a few famous TV title themes) picked because the song is worth knowing on its own, independent of the scene it's attached to.
